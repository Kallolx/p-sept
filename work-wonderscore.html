Search is shifting from ten blue links to a single AI answer. Wonderscore exists to make sure a brand is the one AI models actually cite - and I worked on the backend systems that make that measurable.

The Challenge

You can't optimize for being recommended by ChatGPT if you can't even see whether it's happening.

Traditional SEO tools track rankings on a results page that fewer buyers actually scroll through. Wonderscore needed a way to query multiple AI engines the way a real buyer would, capture whether and how a brand gets cited, and turn that into something a marketing team could act on - continuously, not as a one-off audit.

The Approach

Six coordinated agents - research, writing, community, backlinks, GEO structuring, and analytics - running on one Python backend.

As a Python developer on the team, I worked on the backend services powering this agent pipeline: crawling a site's structure and citations, running commercial-intent queries against AI engines to detect citations, and feeding results into an analytics layer that tracks AI mention share over time. The Writer and GEO agents turn citation gaps into publish-ready, AI-answer-structured content that syncs straight to a merchant's CMS.

The Result

A single dashboard replacing a marketing hire, an SEO agency, and a backlink service.

Wonderscore now runs continuous AI-engine query tracking, deep SEO/GEO audits, and automated content publishing for brands including Coutts, The Ritz London, and Selfridges - condensing what used to take a full marketing team into one workspace.
